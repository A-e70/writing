# Writing

Notes on things I built and what went wrong while building them.

**[My messages were on my own disk and I could not read them](signal-desktop-locked-me-out.md)**
Signal Desktop was deregistered by the server, which cleared its credentials and made every network screen useless. The official fix, relinking, wipes the local database. How the three layers of encryption fit together, the CBC padding detail that silently truncates files, and why "nine files missing" was not information until I found out what the nine were.

**[The detector found nothing, and reported a clean run](a-detector-that-found-nothing.md)**
A face detector at a sensible looking confidence threshold found faces in zero of eighty photographs of people, and exited cleanly. On the failure mode where absence is a legitimate result, so nothing announces that anything went wrong.
